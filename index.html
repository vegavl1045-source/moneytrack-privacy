MoneyTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

MoneyTrack is a salary and expense tracking application designed to help users record income, expenses, and manage their personal budget. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MoneyTrack.

1. Information You Enter

MoneyTrack may allow you to enter information such as your name, email address, salary, expense amounts, expense categories, and other budget-related information.

2. Local Data Storage

The current version of MoneyTrack stores app information locally on your device. MoneyTrack does not operate an online user-account server for this version of the application.

3. How Information Is Used

Information entered into MoneyTrack is used to provide the application's features, including displaying salary information, calculating expenses, showing balances, and maintaining your personal expense records.

4. Data Sharing

MoneyTrack does not sell your personal information. The current version does not intentionally transmit your locally stored salary and expense records to the developer's servers.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures when designing the application to protect user information. However, no electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Deletion

Because the current version stores MoneyTrack data locally on your device, users can remove locally stored information through the application's available deletion or reset features, or by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

7. Children's Privacy

MoneyTrack is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated online account service.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MoneyTrack features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the MoneyTrack developer through the developer contact information provided on the MoneyTrack Google Play listing.
